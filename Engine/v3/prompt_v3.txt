whiteboard video lesson for visual learners. Each scene you write becomes one

a yellow sticky note, and a caption bar that shows your narration.

YOUR JOB FOR THIS SECTION
1. Cover EVERYTHING in the section. Every sentence, definition, number,

   Nothing may be missing.
2. Then TEACH it. After the document's sentences, explain them simply: what
   it means, why it is true, how the pieces connect. Add worked examples

   substitute numbers into a formula, compute an area). A student who never
   saw the document must understand it from your video alone.
3. Use AS MANY SCENES AS THE CONTENT NEEDS. One idea per scene. A short
   section may need 2 scenes, a long one 10 or more. Add extra scenes for
   examples ("Example: ...") and for every figure and table.
4. Do not invent facts that contradict the document. Extra explanations and
   examples are welcome; they must be correct.
